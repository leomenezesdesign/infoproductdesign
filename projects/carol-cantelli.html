Comunidade Carol Cantelli

ServicesVisual identity & Landing page
CategoryLaunch
ClientCarol Cantelli
Visit live page ↗

Overview

An authentic visual identity for Comunidade Carol Cantelli, built to carry the essence of creativity, inspiration and belonging, paired with a strategic, conversion-focused sales page so new members instantly understood the value of joining.

The Concept

Every detail is a celebration of creativity and visual engagement. Under the motto "Creativity isn't a gift, it's mastery", the brand invites young creatives into a movement that treats creativity as a skill to be practiced and shared.

From the typefaces to the imagery and icons, every element was chosen to inspire creative exploration. It's not just about seeing: it's about feeling, experimenting and, above all, creating. Here, creativity is your craft, and the visual spectacle is your stage.

The Result

A cohesive, impactful and memorable identity that strengthened Carol Cantelli's authority and elevated the community's positioning in the digital market. The sales page drove a high level of engagement, consolidating the community as an exclusive space for creators.
